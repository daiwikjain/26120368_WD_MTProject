01 / A little about me

Most of what I know, I learnt by paying attention.

I grew up around a family that makes and exports gemstone jewellery. Since Class XII I've been part of the business, looking after our clients abroad and our online store. It has taught me that people buy from people they trust, and that trust takes time.

I was always the kid asking how things work, and how life works. That curiosity led me to philosophy, then to Vedic Jyotish. Five years on, I've studied more than 150 charts and consulted over 50 people, all through word of mouth. With so much noise online, I'd like to help people my age see astrology as a thoughtful discipline rather than a stereotype. I like to think you don't choose astrology; it chooses you.

Away from work you'll find me on the track, writing poetry, or with my headphones on.
